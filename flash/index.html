Flash ZHAC

ZHAC is a Zigbee hub that runs entirely on an ESP32: coordinator, device library, rules, Lua, MQTT and the web UI. Plug your board in over USB, press a button, done. This needs Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer.

The ready-made hub — Espressif ESP32-S31 Function-CoreBoard

checking for a release…

One board, about $25, nothing to solder or wire: Ethernet, the Zigbee radio, a status LED and USB-C are all on it. This is the build we recommend.

  1. Connect the board's USB-to-UART USB-C port (the one next to the RJ45 jack) to this computer.
  2. Press Install, pick the serial port, wait about two minutes.
  3. Plug in Ethernet and open http://zhac.local. The first visit asks you to set a password.

Updating later happens from the web UI (Settings → Update); it keeps your devices, rules and settings. The status LED blinks green while the join window is open and flashes blue on Zigbee traffic.

Wired hub — ESP32-P4 board with Ethernet

checking for a release…

For the Guition JC-ESP32P4-M3-DEV and other ESP32-P4 boards with an Ethernet jack.

Check the chip first. This build runs on ESP32-P4 silicon v0.x–v1.x only. Run esptool --port <PORT> flash-id and look for ESP32-P4 (revision v1.3). A v3.x chip will not boot it.

Afterwards: plug in Ethernet and open http://zhac.local. The on-module ESP32-C6 also needs the radio firmware below, once.

Zigbee radio for the wired P4 board — ESP32-C6

checking for a release…

The P4 has no radio of its own; the ESP32-C6 next to it is the Zigbee radio and needs this firmware once. It is flashed through the C6's own serial pins, not the board's USB port:

  1. Hold the board's BOOT button, tap RESET, release BOOT. The P4 now waits in download mode and stops resetting the C6.
  2. Connect a 3.3 V USB-serial adapter to the C6 pins on the expansion header: adapter TX → C6_U0RXD, RX → C6_U0TXD, GND → GND. Leave 3.3 V unconnected.
  3. Hold C6_IO9 to GND while you pulse C6_CHIP_PU (reset), then press the button below and pick the adapter's port.

Header labels come from the Guition schematic; check yours before wiring.

Dual-chip hub — ESP32-S3 + ESP32-P4

Two boards joined over SPI, with a CC2652P stick as the radio. Wiring is in the hardware guide. Flash each board separately.

Right after the S3 install, the installer asks for your Wi-Fi name and password and hands them to the hub over the USB cable, then links you to its web UI. This needs the board's UART port; on a board with two USB sockets, use the one marked UART. Reopen this page and press the S3 button again later to change Wi-Fi or find the hub.

If you skip that step, the S3 opens a Wi-Fi network called ZHAC-XXXX; its password is printed on the S3's serial console at boot. Join it and pick your home network.
